Lightmapping help

Projection mapping and video mapping that runs in your phone’s browser. You don’t need a laptop or an App Store install, and your projects stay on your device.

Install on iPhone or iPad

  • Open this site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Open it from the home screen icon and it runs full screen like an app.

Connect a projector

  • Cable: plug a USB-C or Lightning HDMI adapter into your phone, then tap ⛶ for clean full-screen output. Double-tap to come back to the controls.
  • AirPlay: use Screen Mirroring from Control Center, then tap ⛶.
  • Controls on your phone, output on the projector: open this site on the device plugged into the projector (laptop, TV browser or spare tablet) and choose Receiver mode. On your phone, go to Export & Link, enter the receiver’s code and tap Live output.

Map a surface

  • Tap ＋ and choose a square, circle, text, box/cube, a hand-drawn outline, or a shape cut from a photo.
  • Drag the yellow corners onto the real-world corners. Drag the centre handle to move, and use two fingers to scale and rotate.
  • To wrap curved objects, set Warp grid to 3×3 or more and drag the extra orange handles.
  • ✂︎ Cut out draws a hole, for example around a door or a window frame. Soft edge feathers the border.

Depth mode

Choose Depth shape from photo, pick a photo of the object and tap it. Lightmapping cuts the object out and builds a depth map. Depth-aware visuals such as Topographic and Depth sweep then follow its form. Turn on AI depth to use a neural depth model (it downloads about 30 MB the first time). Without it, depth is estimated from the shape.

Visuals, effects and control

  • The library has 88 looks, including 52 for Halloween and Christmas. You can also use your own photos and videos, the live camera, or hand drawings that animate.
  • Every slider has a modulation menu: audio level, bass, mid or high; four tempo-synced LFOs; envelopes; macros; and hand and face tracking. MIDI learn works in browsers that support Web MIDI (Chrome, Edge, Android).
  • Scenes switch with a cut, fade or dissolve. Perform (🎛) gives you big scene pads, tap tempo and macros.

Build and export a show

  • Timeline (⏱): add clips of scenes, choose an audio track, and add automation lanes that drive macros.
  • Export (⤓) records the show in 720p, 1080p or 4K with audio. On iPhone, choose Save Video in the share sheet.
  • Send show transfers it to a receiver, which saves it and loops it. You can close the app and take your phone away; the receiver keeps playing and resumes the show after a restart.

Limits

  • Safari on iPhone doesn’t support Web MIDI.
  • Exports record in real time, so keep the tab open until the export finishes.
  • Linking and the optional AI models need an internet connection. Everything else works offline once the app has loaded.
